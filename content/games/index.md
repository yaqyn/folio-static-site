# Games

Step into a rainy forest and meet its wildlings, or pilot an upgradeable ship through incoming asteroids. These games began as Boot.dev projects and grew through independent work.

## Clibble — Terminal RPG

![Clibble — Terminal RPG](/images/projects/clibble-cover.webp)

A little wilderness RPG that opens inside your terminal. Choose with arrows and Enter from a clearing framed by green pixel trees, soft rain, and drifting motes. Travel six trails, meet ten original wildlings, and offer catch seeds to add their stories to your field journal. The world works entirely offline.

Catches and escapes consume saved supplies and tries: twelve seeds, replenishing over time, and three tries per wildling in a thirty-minute window. Restarting or changing trails keeps those limits. Motion can be switched off, and scriptable commands remain available alongside the RPG screen. An optional read-only web companion lets you search your journal by name or habitat. These are local single-player rules; the save file and device clock are trusted.

[Explore Clibble on GitHub](https://github.com/yaqyn/clibble-terminal-rpg)

## Orbit — Asteroids Game

![Orbit — Asteroids Game](/images/projects/orbit-cover.webp)

A Python and Pygame arcade game with an upgradeable starfighter, rotating shaded asteroid meshes, and momentum-based flight. Collect salvage to choose modules, recharge shields between impacts, and chain kills for bonus points. Pilot ranks and escalating contracts give the run a progression loop.

A 1280 × 720 logical canvas scales into a resizable window or fullscreen display while preserving its proportions. Keyboard controls cover flight, boost, upgrades, pause, restart, and fullscreen. Persistent best scores and reduced-motion options remain available.

Run `./launch` from the repository. The first launch provisions its locked environment and needs internet access; later launches reuse it.

[Explore Orbit on GitHub](https://github.com/yaqyn/orbit-asteroids-game)
